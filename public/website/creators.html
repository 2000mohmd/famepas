Questions
Does it cost anything?+

No. FamePass is free for creators.

Who can join?+

Creators based in Lebanon. Every profile is reviewed before approval.

What do I have to post?+

Posts, Reels and Stories from your visit, tagging the venue. Each experience lists exactly what's expected.

When do I have to post?+

By the deadline listed in your offer. Creators who post on time get priority access to new experiences.

What if I can't make my booking?+

Cancel in the app before your visit. Repeated no-shows can lead to removal from FamePass.

Can I bring someone?+

It depends on the venue. Each experience shows exactly what's included, including any plus-one.

Do I get paid?+

FamePass works on experiences, not cash. You get the visit, the perks and the exposure.

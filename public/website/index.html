05Questions

Good to know.

More detail on the creators and venues pages.

What is FamePass?+

An app that matches creators with venues in Lebanon. Venues offer an experience, creators enjoy it and share it with their audience.

Is it free for creators?+

Yes. Creators join for free once their profile is approved.

What does it cost a venue?+

Venues pay one monthly subscription. No cash goes to creators — the experience is the exchange.

Who handles the communication?+

We do. FamePass coordinates every booking and message between venue and creator.

Where does FamePass operate?+

Across Lebanon — Beirut, Mount Lebanon, the North and the South.
